Project

OptimisedEats

A free budget nutrition app for Australian, New Zealand, and American families — personalised nutrient targets, recipes ranked by nutrition per dollar, meal planning, and shopping lists. Built solo, using Claude as the development partner, alongside a full-time job.

Open the app → Listen to the podcast
What's inside the app

One connected tool — set up your household once, and everything below works off the same nutrient targets and meal plan.

👤
Household Profiles
Add everyone who eats together — age, sex, and diet type set personalised RDI targets for each person.
📖
Recipe Builder
Browse recipes ranked by nutrition per dollar, and boost them with cheap extras like liver, eggs, or seeds.
📅
Daily & Weekly Planner
Build today's meals or plan a full week ahead, one recipe at a time.
📊
Nutrient Dashboard
See live coverage against your personal RDI targets, and what's still short.
🛒
Shopping List
Auto-generated from your planned meals, with quantities calculated for you.
📚
Learn
Plain-language nutrition education, tailored to your household's ages and stages.
A guide library most nutrition apps don't bother with

Every guide is sourced from a national health body and priced against real supermarket data for the country you're actually shopping in — not generic "eat your veggies" advice.

34+
in-depth guides, from pre-conception to elderly nutrition
106
budget-optimised recipes, filterable by diet and nutrient focus
2
fully localised regions — not just a currency swap

🇦🇺 AU / NZ edition

  • Reference values from NHMRC (Australia's national health body)
  • Pricing benchmarked against Coles, Woolworths, and Aldi
  • Region-specific guides — e.g. kangaroo meat as a budget protein, iodine deficiency resurgence in AU

🇺🇸 US edition

  • Every reference value rebuilt from NIH ODS, not converted from the AU numbers
  • Pricing benchmarked against Kroger and Aldi US
  • Same depth, independently re-sourced — a separate guide, not a translation

A sample of what's covered:

⚠️
Nutrient Gap Data

Life-Stage Guides
