VoltageDrop

A standalone voltage drop calculator covering four countries' wiring standards in one tool — AS/NZS 3000 & AS 3008 (Australia), NEC (US), IS 732 (India), and SANS 10142 (South Africa). Free, no signup, built solo alongside a full-time job in the lighting industry.
